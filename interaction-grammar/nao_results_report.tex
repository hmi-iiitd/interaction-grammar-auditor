\section{Introduction}
This report presents the evaluation of the Interaction Contract Auditor on a dataset of 12 interaction scenarios involving a NAO robot and a human participant. The scenarios span three primary families: Delivery and Acknowledgment, Interruption and Turn-Taking, and Repair and Timeout.

The objective is to verify the auditor's ability to detect contract violations, accurately attribute the failure to the responsible agent, and provide high-fidelity counterexamples.

\section{Dataset Overview}
The dataset consists of 12 curated scenarios designed to test edge cases of interaction grammar.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Delivery Family (A1--A4)}: Tests for missing or late acknowledgments and confirmations.
    \item \textbf{Interruption Family (B1--B4)}: Tests for floor-yielding and interruption acknowledgment.
    \item \textbf{Repair Family (C1--C4)}: Tests for retry limits, repair exhaustion, and global timeouts.
\end{itemize}

The complete dataset, including ROS db3 files, extracted traces, and locked contracts, is available at: \\
\url{https://github.com/[username]/[repo]/tree/main/interaction-grammar/dataset_nao}
